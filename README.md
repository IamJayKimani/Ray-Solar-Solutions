Ray Solar Solutions - Solar Lighting Web Application

 Project Overview
Ray Solar Solutions is a role-based web application designed to revolutionize the solar lighting industry by connecting customers, providers, and administrators on a single, efficient platform. The application addresses the critical need for accessible, sustainable lighting solutions in rural and off-grid areas by streamlining product discovery, sales, and support processes.

 Key Features
Three-Tier Role System: Customers, Providers, and Administrators with distinct permissions

Product Management: Browse, search, and filter solar lighting products

E-Commerce Functionality: Shopping cart and integrated payment system

Analytics Dashboard: Sales metrics and customer engagement insights

Support System: Dedicated customer service portal with ticket management

Administrative Controls: User verification, product moderation, and platform oversight
